# Source Policy

Every material factual claim must be traceable to a source.

## Source tiers

- `Primary`: company IR, SEC filings, official transcripts, regulator publications
- `Independent`: Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ, AP, major trade reporting
- `Reference`: quote pages, screeners, finance portals

## Minimums

- Full memo: 3 or more distinct clickable links, with at least 1 primary source
- Short update: 2 or more distinct clickable links, with at least 1 primary or independent source

## Rules

- Pair material claims with primary or independent sources whenever possible.
- Keep links close to the factual claim they support.
- Values inside `Derived Metrics` can cite themselves through formulas instead of external links.
- For synthetic or assumption-based scenarios, do not attach generic market homepages just to satisfy a citation aesthetic. Either use real issuer-specific evidence or say that the scenario is assumption-based and source-light.
