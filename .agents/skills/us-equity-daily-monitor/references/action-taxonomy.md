# Action Taxonomy

Choose monitor status before writing prose.

## Monitor Status

- `No Material Change`: the latest evidence does not materially alter the thesis or risk/reward. Keep the artifact short and explicit.
- `Watch Closely`: something relevant changed, but confirmation is still missing. Show the exact thresholds that would upgrade or downgrade the call.
- `Review Now`: the evidence materially changes the decision now. This does not automatically mean `Buy`; it can also justify `Avoid for now`, `Trim`, or `Exit`.

## Decision Card fields

- `Monitor Status`: `No Material Change` | `Watch Closely` | `Review Now`
- `New Money Action`: `Buy` | `Starter Only` | `Wait` | `Avoid for now`
- `Existing Holder Action`: `Add` | `Hold` | `Hold/Do not add` | `Trim` | `Exit`
- `Thesis Impact`: `No material change` | `Positive` | `Mixed` | `Negative`
- `Signal Quality`: `Weak` | `Moderate` | `Strong`
- `Confidence`: `Low` | `Medium` | `High`

## Calibration rules

- `No Material Change` means shorter output, not safer-sounding output.
- `Watch Closely` is for mixed but relevant change with concrete confirmation triggers, not filler prose.
- `Review Now` is for cases where the user should act or re-underwrite now.
- `Wait`, `Hold`, and `Hold/Do not add` are allowed only if the artifact includes explicit `Upgrade / Review Now`, `Downgrade / De-risk`, and `Invalidation` triggers.
- Do not force a positive recommendation. `Review Now + Avoid for now` and `Review Now + Exit` are valid outcomes.
