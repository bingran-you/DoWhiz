## Why Now

The new guidance revision matters now because it changes the near-term operating path that investors are underwriting, not just the long-run quality story. This is a `Review Now` signal because the key question is whether the raised expectations are still conservative, already fully priced, or vulnerable to the next margin datapoint.

## Dual-Horizon Framing

### Near-Term Timing View (next 1-2 quarters)
The next print is the dominant catalyst because it can confirm whether the raised demand view is translating into revenue and gross-margin durability at the same time. If revenue beats but margin slips, the signal stays positive but the urgency falls from "review now" to "watch closely."

### Long-Term Ownership View (multi-year)
The multi-year AI infrastructure thesis still looks intact because hyperscaler spending, inference demand, and ecosystem lock-in remain supportive. Long-term ownership depends less on one quarter and more on whether NVIDIA can sustain pricing power and platform breadth as customers optimize their capex.

## Scenarios

### Bull Case
**Trigger:** Revenue above $70B and gross margin above 74%. The signal becomes decisively positive because the company would be proving that higher demand is converting cleanly into profitable growth.

### Base Case
**Trigger:** Revenue stays near the current run-rate and gross margin holds in the low-to-mid 70s. The signal remains positive, but the setup becomes less urgent because expectations and results stay mostly aligned.

### Bear Case
**Invalidation:** Revenue misses the raised setup or gross margin slips below 71%. The signal weakens because the new optimism would no longer be supported by operating evidence.

## What Would Change The View

- **Upgrade / Review Now:** Revenue above $70B with gross margin above 74%, or a new guide that lifts the annual run-rate by more than 5%.
- **Downgrade / De-risk:** Gross margin below 71%, or a guide cut that reduces next-quarter expectations by 5% or more.
- **Invalidation:** A customer capex reset or export-control change that threatens more than 10% of expected revenue.

## Judgment

*Inference, Medium confidence.* This is a positive `Review Now` signal because the new information directly changes the operating assumptions that justify the stock's valuation. Confidence stays at Medium rather than High because the market is already pricing in aggressive execution, so the main open question is not "is NVIDIA a good company?" but "is the new upside still underappreciated?"
